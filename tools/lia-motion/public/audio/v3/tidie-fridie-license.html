Royalty Free Funky Bass Music

Tidie Fridie leads with an unusually strong low-end – two bass guitars provide a fat, squidgy foundation that gives the track its primary character and a great deal of its personality. The track is jolly on the surface but, as its own description warns, not afraid to get serious when it needs to – a flexibility that makes it more useful than straightforward upbeat background music, and rather more interesting.

Also a solid choice for podcast background audio, social media content, and any creator looking for something with genuine character that works across a range of contexts without imposing a single mood.
