Royalty Free Summer Dance Music

Sunsetter exists precisely at the intersection of chilled and motivated – too relaxed to be driving electronic music, but with enough rhythmic energy to keep things moving with genuine purpose. A quirky ukulele hook provides the melodic identity with bright, playful character, a deep bassline gives the track its low-end cool, and driving drums keep the forward momentum going without disrupting the beach-adjacent vibe. An acoustic guitar features because, as the original notes accurately observe, there is always an idiot with a guitar at the beach – and this one is rather good. The whole thing has the quality of a perfect afternoon that knows it’s perfect and is entirely relaxed about it.

Perfect For:

Works brilliantly for summer lifestyle content, beach and outdoor videos, and any travel production set somewhere warm where a chilled, motivated groove creates the right atmosphere. Effective for party and social content, poolside and festival coverage, and any YouTube production where sunshine energy without aggressive BPM is the requirement. Also a solid choice for food and drink content with a warm-weather setting, and for any project where the music should feel like a good holiday rather than a sprint.
