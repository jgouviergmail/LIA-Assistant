# ADR-359 — Longitudinal discussion context and user control

**Status:** Accepted — implemented on the native application contracts; qualification boundaries below.
**Date:** 2026-10-20
**Deciders:** Product owner, with inline engineering analysis.
**Scope:** Personal source evidence, longitudinal understanding, exclusions and directives.

## Context and problem statement

A past medical appointment and a monthly medical appointment are different
contexts. A recent notification feed, unread-only email list or first page of
memories cannot establish that distinction. Conversely, repeatedly searching
every source is costly and still does not establish identity, recurrence or
whether a scheduled event actually happened.

The person explicitly requires contextual intelligence, a consultable derived
knowledge base, purge and reversible **Non utilisable** controls. The portrait
« Comment LIA te perçoit » is an autonomous source accepted or refused as a whole.
Corrections stored in « Règles et directives » must govern future initiative.

## Decision drivers

- Distinguish source facts, intentions, interpretations and hypotheses.
- Preserve source identity, permissions, time semantics and incomplete coverage.
- Reuse understood evidence without building an opaque, independent personal truth.
- Apply human exclusions before retrieval, pattern computation and model use.
- Make every derived state accountable to its current supports and generation.

## Considered options

| Option | Assessment |
|---|---|
| Recent-context prompt without persistent understanding | Simple, but cannot qualify longitudinal evidence and repeats expensive searches; rejected. |
| General semantic memory as the entire context store | Reuses storage, but mixes explicit memory with disposable derived state and loses purge/coverage semantics; rejected. |
| New external graph database | Expressive relations, but adds operations and permissions machinery before demonstrating a need; deferred. |
| Typed observations, episodes and supported patterns on PostgreSQL | Uses existing persistence and explicit support edges, with inspectable updates and invalidation; selected. |

## Decision outcome

Keep a dedicated **derived** context store inside the discussion domain, backed
by the existing PostgreSQL service. Represent minimal source observations,
consolidated episodes, qualified patterns and their support relationships with
strict schemas. Do not store complete email or SMS bodies merely to avoid future
source access. Source material remains external evidence, never instructions.

The shared [source contracts](../../apps/api/src/domains/shared/discussion_sources.py)
and [catalogue](../../apps/api/src/domains/shared/discussion_catalogue.py) publish
the closed vocabulary, operations, dependencies and scope. Each read returns
its effective interval, status, truncation, exact total when known, source
revision and semantic references. The last page of a targeted or cursored read
is not proof of complete historical coverage. Native permissions and source
availability are checked independently of the discussion switch.

Separate recording time from occurrence time; a captured SMS date is not a
receipt date, a journal entry is an interpretation, and a planned reminder or
meeting action does not prove completion. Preserve native recurrence rules,
calendar-local dates, exceptions and source/container identity at their actual
adapter boundary. A periodic pattern needs sufficient consistent support;
absence, incomplete history and silence do not establish missed events, illness
or distress. Identity resolution requires authorized corroboration and retains
ambiguity rather than merging names speculatively.

Update understood context incrementally by semantic version and source delta.
Keep a single supported episode for the same real-world subject when evidence
permits; do not convert a question previously generated by LIA into a human fact.
Recheck cached support authority and expiry before model use and publication,
then invalidate and recalculate affected derivatives after source correction,
deletion, expiry or consent withdrawal.

### Portrait as an independent whole

The portrait is its own source root, governed by its current native permission
and the dedicated discussion switch. **Do not inspect or refilter its historical
supports according to other discussion source switches.** This is the product
owner's explicit choice. The block remains a dated interpretation, not proof of
the person's present feelings. If a known indivisible block would reintroduce
an explicitly excluded item, omit that whole block for the calculation; do not
silently rewrite it or invent a historical provenance graph.

### Visible context, purge and non-usable elements

The eventual management interface lists understood elements, their source and
period, support status and usage choice. It can purge a selection or the whole
derived base, mark elements **Non utilisable**, and explicitly restore their use.
The person retains access to management while conversational initiative is off.

Persist usage exclusions separately from rebuilt generations, keyed by stable
semantic identity. Apply them before pattern denominators, retrieval, context
compilation, decision and writing. A regenerated ID, changed summary or known
alternate support path must not silently lift the same exclusion. A broader
topic refusal is a separate control, never inferred from a single item's flag.

Purge increments an owner generation, invalidates dependent candidates and
workers, and removes the selected derived contents. Keep only minimal exclusion
references needed to respect the person's usage choices. Prevent immediate
historical reimport with a resume marker; historical reconstruction is explicit
and still respects exclusions. A stale worker cannot republish a purged
generation. Source originals and general memory keep their native commands.

### Directives and immediate corrections

Read a complete coherent snapshot of active procedural memories, independently
of the factual-memory source toggle and semantic top-k. Global memory consent
still applies. Preserve native creation-time pinning and manual correction
rights described in [ADR-236](ADR-236-procedural-memory.md).

Rules constrain behavior; they are not subjects to discuss. A derived constraint
cache remains linked to canonical memory identity and version. If applicable
rules cannot fit the bounded context, reduce candidates or defer rather than
drop a rule silently. Do not change protected pinned memories automatically to
resolve a contradiction.

A clear human correction immediately invalidates relevant pending initiative
through a transient guard linked to the actual reply. Native memory extraction
may subsequently persist a directive and replace that guard with its receipt.
An asynchronous extraction failure cannot be reported as permanent learning.
« Not now », a durable topic refusal and an unexplained negative reaction have
different semantics; uncertainty defers the subject instead of inventing a rule.

## Consequences

Understanding is reusable and user-controllable, at the cost of explicit support
graphs, generation fencing and invalidation paths. Retention cannot outlive a
shorter-lived source. Account export and erasure cover observations, episodes,
patterns, exclusions, constraints and resume markers. Conversation reset removes
its supports without arbitrarily deleting episodes still supported elsewhere.

Retention is bounded by each source and the [context retention policy](../../apps/api/src/domains/proactive_discussions/context_store/preparation.py); [expiration maintenance](../../apps/api/src/domains/proactive_discussions/context_store/maintenance.py) runs even when generation is off. It preserves explicit non-use choices.

## Implementation state and verification

All declared sources are installed through [native composition](../../apps/api/src/infrastructure/proactive/discussion_sources.py). The typed [context store](../../apps/api/src/domains/proactive_discussions/context_store/) contains body-free source proofs, supported observations, episodes and qualified patterns. Native recurrence and occurrence identity remain distinct from attendance. Observation and episode qualifications cannot upgrade plans or interpretations to human testimony.

Complete procedural rules and pending first-party corrections are read independently of factual-memory source selection. A restrictive read-rule compiler runs before source-body access; its body-free signature cache is invalidated by changed controls. A prior insufficient interpretation cannot become ready through cache reuse. Explicit reconstruction forces reinterpretation, while exact evidence versions fence warm reuse.

The settings interface provides a bounded, searchable and filtered context view, source/type/usability/date filters, exact purge preview, reversible exclusions and retained exclusions after purge. The same commands and filters are available through internal tools with native approval. Expiration maintenance runs independently of producer availability; source removal, conversation reset and account erasure invalidate the declared derived data.

[Context PostgreSQL tests](../../apps/api/tests/integration/domains/proactive_discussions/test_context_store_pg.py), [pattern tests](../../apps/api/tests/unit/domains/proactive_discussions/test_longitudinal_patterns.py), [qualification guards](../../apps/api/tests/unit/domains/proactive_discussions/test_qualification_ceiling.py) and [native source integration tests](../../apps/api/tests/integration/domains/proactive_discussions/) cover these contracts. A model's self-rating is not evidence of naturalness; real-provider and human assessment are separate from deterministic qualification.

## Amendment 2026-10-21 — bounded guards, explicit deferral, visible rebuilds

- **A reply belongs to the opening it answers only while that opening is open**
  ([replies](../../apps/api/src/domains/proactive_discussions/replies.py)): a message written
  after the deadline is never attached to it, and an opening past its deadline
  with no reply expires. Before, the first later message, however unrelated,
  became its reply and a restriction on every later run.
- **The transient guard is transient**: an unacknowledged reply restrains later
  runs for at most `DISCUSSION_REPLY_GUARD_TTL_HOURS`. An extraction that never
  comes can neither hold the producer for ever nor become permanent learning.
- **Controls that cannot be read whole defer the run explicitly**: no claim, no
  spend, outcome `controls_over_budget`, logged and counted, and stated in the
  settings as the reason a requested rebuild waits. It used to escape the runner
  and kill that owner's producer silently.
- **A support is a whole statement.** A procedural memory with its nuance and topic,
  or a reply of a few sentences, exceeded a 256-character field bound: every later
  run of that owner failed validation before its claim. The field is now bounded
  by `CONTROL_SUPPORT_MAX_CHARS` ([rule controls](../../apps/api/src/domains/proactive_discussions/rule_controls.py)),
  the token budgets bounding the set.
- **The read-rule compiler has its own system prompt** (`discussion_rules_prompt`),
  never the context prompt whose instructions it contradicted; the reply guard's
  scaffold lives in `discussion_control_lines`.
- **A rebuild is visible while it runs, and what it cost.** A second request while
  one is pending changes nothing and fences off nothing. `GET
  /proactive-discussions/activity` ([read model](../../apps/api/src/infrastructure/proactive/discussion_activity.py))
  publishes whether a rebuild is requested or running, the server-side obstacle
  that holds it, the last rebuild with the cost of its own ledger row, and today's
  allowance by the reservation's own predicate — in euros at the pricing cache's
  rate, the instance's room as a boolean only. A rebuild's spend is filed under the
  person's authorship (`user`), never as LIA's initiative.

## Amendment 2026-10-21 b — a context built from the configured slot, and a rebuild that covers

Measured on the owner's production account before anything changed, through the
run's own readers and prompt measure ([harness](../../apps/api/scripts/proactive_discussions/measure_context.py),
`task discussions:context:measure`, counts only): 19 pages, 58 admitted facts, and a
first context call of 8,135 tokens against a fixed ceiling of 8,000 — not one fact
could be offered, so the rebuild interpreted nothing and said it was done.

- **The prompt ceiling is the configured slot's**: its window, less its output budget
  and one framing margin ([prompt measure](../../apps/api/src/domains/proactive_discussions/llm_contracts.py)).
  No envelope is written in the code any more; a model is whatever an administrator
  configured (ADR-244, ADR-278). Measured after: the 58 facts fit, with ~891,000
  tokens to spare on a 1,000,000-token slot.
- **An interpretation is reused while what its meaning depends on holds**: its
  checkpoint carries a signature of the readable sources, the consented and refused
  topics, the clock and the language ([checkpoints](../../apps/api/src/domains/proactive_discussions/context_store/checkpoints.py)),
  never the policy's revision counter. Editing the hours, the daily limit or a pause —
  or switching discussions off and on — no longer has every fact interpreted, and
  paid for, again.
- **A rebuild covers.** It moves the context epoch, so every fact is due once; each
  pass checkpoints what it analysed and the next offers the rest
  ([store](../../apps/api/src/infrastructure/proactive/discussion_context.py)). Before, while a
  rebuild was requested every pass was handed every fact and re-offered the same
  first ones: two calls, 12 distinct facts of 58. A covering build does not stop at
  its first ready pass ([loop](../../apps/api/src/domains/proactive_discussions/contextualization/loop.py)):
  it keeps that ready context, stores it again at the end (each pass's publication
  clears it), and reads on while facts remain. A rebuild has its own call budget,
  `DISCUSSION_REBUILD_CONTEXT_CALLS` (8); an opening keeps
  `DISCUSSION_CONTEXT_CALLS_PER_RUN` (3), since it needs enough to speak, not
  everything. An interrupted rebuild resumes where it stopped instead of paying again.
- **Measured on production, dry and on the real context slot** (nothing written to
  the discussion tables, the spend billed to the account): 63 of 63 facts interpreted
  in 6 calls, one of them asking for a research wave, 11 proposals, €0.26, 57 s —
  against 2 proposals before. Thirty facts a pass covered everything in 2 calls for
  the same cost but produced 4 proposals instead of 6 at twelve: the prompt asks each
  call for a few compact proposals, so the context's richness follows the number of
  calls. `DISCUSSION_CONTEXT_FACTS_PER_PASS` therefore stays 12.
- **Known limit, written down rather than repaired by a constant**: the prompt
  measure under-counts a provider's tokenizer by about a third (11,997 measured,
  16,134 billed, same request). A large window absorbs it; a small one keeps less
  real margin than the ceiling suggests, and a refusal there fails the run visibly.

## Amendment 2026-10-21 c — a proposal is refused alone, and the current weather is one object

Measured on the owner's dev account the same day, once amendment b let rebuilds reach
their context calls:

- **Five rebuilds in a row stopped on `evidence_changed`, in 4 to 11 seconds, before
  any call**: the revalidation read the weather again and never found it. The provider
  stamps the current conditions with the instant it answers (measured: reads 46 and
  91 seconds apart moved the stamp by exactly that, every value unchanged), and the
  fact took that stamp as its identity, so every read made a new object. The current
  conditions are now ONE object per provider and place, observed, versioned by what
  they say ([weather source](../../apps/api/src/domains/connectors/discussion_weather.py));
  a forecast point is identified by the instant it forecasts, and is planned. A known
  fact is read again by the read that can return it — one rule for the revalidation
  and for the stored proofs, `reread_operation`
  ([catalogue](../../apps/api/src/domains/shared/discussion_catalogue.py)): a planned
  fact of a source with no detail read is read in its future, where a forecast is,
  never in a recent page that cannot hold it. Unchanged weather is no longer
  interpreted, and paid for, again at every run.
- **Four rebuilds failed whole on one configured model**: one to four proposals per
  answer broke the rules the contract enforces — an anchor among its own references,
  each reference once, one reference for an observation — and each refused the whole
  interpretation; the model refuses a forced tool call, so nothing retried. Those rules
  were enforced and published nowhere (ADR-184's trap): the context prompt now states
  them. Whatever model a slot runs, a proposal is read on its own
  ([reading](../../apps/api/src/domains/proactive_discussions/contextualization/proposal_reading.py)):
  what is mechanical is repaired (a reference listed twice or left empty, an anchor
  missing from its own references, and an observation resting on several facts, which
  by the store's own definition is an episode: the two kinds differ in nothing else —
  measured the same afternoon, a rebuild's only proposal was such an observation, and
  refusing it left the rebuild with nothing), the rest is set aside alone — beyond the
  published cap, any other shape the contract refuses. A classification and the
  resolution stay strict: dropping a topic label would read a sensitive fact as an
  ordinary one. The schema the model reads is unchanged, byte for byte.
- **The decision is read the same way**: an opening run failed at its decision over a
  skip reason longer than the schema allows. A free text past its published length is
  cut at it (the length read from the schema), an engagement's reference listed twice
  is read once ([decision reading](../../apps/api/src/domains/proactive_discussions/decision_reading.py));
  the action, the references and the delay stay strict.
- **The publication refused the same way, and no run had reached it yet**: one
  proposal qualified beyond its evidence, touching a refused topic, spanning two
  subjects or making an item another proposal of the pass makes failed the whole
  generation ([preparation](../../apps/api/src/domains/proactive_discussions/context_store/preparation.py)),
  and the production measurement had kept its proposals in memory. Each is now
  refused alone under a named reason (`ProposalRefusal`), the others published.
- **Two more answers refused a whole run, measured with these fixes live**: a rebuild
  whose weather revalidation now passed failed on a `ValueError` right after a valid
  answer — a forgotten or doubled topic label
  ([classification](../../apps/api/src/domains/proactive_discussions/contextualization/classification.py)),
  or a research request the policy refuses
  ([loop](../../apps/api/src/domains/proactive_discussions/contextualization/loop.py)).
  Labels still only add topics: a reference labelled twice takes both, a label for a
  fact the call was not shown is ignored, and a fact left unlabelled leaves its pass
  — never read as unlabelled, neither interpreted nor checkpointed, offered again
  later. A refused research request is never read and never refuses the run.
- **A rebuild's read budget serves its reads, and a re-read that was not made is not a
  change**: a rebuild stopped on `evidence_changed` after three passes and 66 physical
  calls against a budget of 64 — every pass re-read each of its facts, one read per
  fact, before and after its call, and the last re-read came back closed for budget.
  Facts asking for the same read now share it
  ([revalidation](../../apps/api/src/infrastructure/proactive/discussion_revalidation.py));
  a re-read the person's controls refuse stops as `evidence_refused`, one out of budget
  as `read_budget_exhausted`, an unreadable one as `evidence_page_missing` — only a
  page that was read and no longer holds the fact at its version is
  `evidence_changed`. A rebuild no longer re-reads its facts around its calls
  ([runner](../../apps/api/src/infrastructure/proactive/discussion_runner.py)): it
  decides nothing, every proof it stores is read again at its version before any later
  use, and eight passes would take hundreds of calls. Its authority is still checked at
  every read and every model call; an opening keeps every revalidation, its decision's
  included.
- **A rebuild cut short resumes, as amendment b said it would**: each pass's
  publication used to clear the rebuild request, so a rebuild stopped after its first
  pass (its deadline, a failure, a restart) was never served again. Only the rebuild's
  own end clears it now ([updater](../../apps/api/src/domains/proactive_discussions/context_store/updater.py));
  its checkpoints keep what it had interpreted from being paid for twice.
- **An interrupted run holds what it may have spent, never its whole reservation**: a
  rebuild cut by a restart had issued 0.09 USD of calls, yet its uncertain settlement
  kept its whole 4.94 USD estimate held, and every later run of the day stopped on
  `budget_exhausted`. An uncertain reservation now holds what it issued and was not
  billed — what `issued_usd` was always documented to mean for crash recovery — and
  releases the rest ([spend](../../apps/api/src/domains/proactive_discussions/finance_repository.py)).
- **A rebuild has its own deadline, and reaching a deadline is a stop, not a failure**:
  the first rebuild through these fixes interpreted 69 facts of 69 in 6 calls, which
  grew from 14 to 53 seconds as each pass showed the model what earlier ones had
  understood: 237 seconds against the opening's 240. `DISCUSSION_REBUILD_DEADLINE_SECONDS`
  bounds a rebuild alone, never below an opening's; it may outlast the lease, which
  every read and every model call renews, so the lease only has to outlast one step. A
  run its deadline stops ends as `deadline_reached`, and a rebuild so cut resumes.
- **A setting never accepts what the contract it feeds refuses**: every rebuild failed
  in under three seconds, before its first read, once `DISCUSSION_READ_CALL_LIMIT` was
  set to 20 — the setting allowed 100 while the read contract stopped at 16, and the
  timeout allowed 120 seconds against 60. One constant now holds each bound for both
  (`DISCUSSION_READ_CALL_LIMIT_MAX`, `DISCUSSION_READ_TIMEOUT_SECONDS_MAX`), and a test
  holds every read bound a setting feeds inside its contract's.
- **What a run set aside is a count**: `stats.proposals_refused`, shown in the
  settings when it is not zero, beside `facts_unclassified` and `research_refused`,
  and one log line per stage (`discussion_context_proposals_refused`, `stage` reading
  or publication) counting the reasons, never the content. A run whose build stops —
  a fact changed, a call failed — keeps the counts it had gathered; the nine runs
  above kept none.

## Amendment 2026-10-21 d — the page shows the last run, an opening is an act, a ceiling is said, retention forgets nothing

Measured on the owner's dev account once amendment c ran, with automatic runs every two
minutes:

- **The figures on screen were not the last run's**: the panel showed the last rebuild —
  its reason, its counts, its 0.032 € — while three automatic runs after it each
  interpreted twelve facts and updated the context. The activity now carries three runs
  ([activity](../../apps/api/src/infrastructure/proactive/discussion_activity.py)): the
  last rebuild (date and state), the last run that **updated** the context — one whose
  publication moved the generation, counted per run as `stats.context_updates`, never
  inferred from a call that may have changed nothing — and the **last run**, whose kind,
  reason, counts and cost the page shows, read from its own `message_token_summary` row.
  The panel re-reads the activity every minute while it is open, and the context list
  once a run updated it.
- **A published run kept no reason and no counts**: the publication settles the run and
  releases the account in its own transaction, so the run's later settlement found no
  claim and wrote nothing — every published opening read « completed », with no reason.
  The publication writes `published` and the counts itself
  ([publication](../../apps/api/src/domains/proactive_discussions/publication_repository.py)).
- **An opening is an act of LIA's own, and the register did not have it**: a run's
  consultations were filed under `proactive`, its act was not, so « Actions menées
  d'elle-même » never showed a discussion. The opening is claimed before its message is
  posted and settled from the commit that posts it — the message is in the conversation
  whatever a push does afterwards — as `proactive_discussion`
  ([effects](../../apps/api/src/domains/agents/effects/out_of_turn_effects.py), ADR-263).
- **A usage limit is asked before a run reads anything**: every model call already asked
  the person's usage limit, but a run started anyway, read its sources (a paid weather
  read included) and was filed `provider_backoff` when its first call came back 429. The
  run now asks the same verdict right after its claim and settles `usage_limit_reached`
  before any read or reservation; a limit reached during the run stops it under the
  same reason. The next tick asks again, so a limit an administrator lifts resumes at
  once. Measured the same evening, every discussion euro reached the account's ledgers:
  0.065886 € of model calls in `token_usage_logs` and as much in `user_statistics`, the
  Google calls likewise.
- **A ceiling is said, never quantified**: the page showed the day's spend, its ceiling,
  the run ceiling and what the next run would hold — amounts a person can act on in no
  way. It now says, in one sentence and only when one stops LIA, which ceiling: the
  instance's daily budget, the person's usage limit, a run ceiling below one call, the
  account's day or the instance's day for discussions — in the order a run meets them,
  with the day it renews when it is a day. A requested rebuild a ceiling holds says so.
- **Retention is lifecycle, never forgetting**: the maintenance job treated an
  observation aging out as a forgetting act — it moved the context epoch, fenced the
  run in progress and expired the pending opening with its undelivered outbox. The
  location observation lives 15 minutes and the current weather 30, so on the owner's
  account the epoch moved three times in an hour: every checkpoint was void each time
  (each opening run re-interpreted twelve facts, the cap, at every tick), a run in
  flight was abandoned, and all three openings of the evening read « expired » 4 to 16
  minutes after LIA wrote them. An expiry now deletes what aged out, moves the
  generation and clears the ready context — nothing else
  ([maintenance](../../apps/api/src/domains/proactive_discussions/context_store/maintenance.py)).
  What a run publishes afterwards rests on facts it reads then, admitted again with
  their own retention, so nothing expired is published. The person's acts — a purge,
  an exclusion, an erased source — still move the epoch.
- **The weather is versioned at the precision a sentence reads it**: a probe reading the
  current conditions every 30 seconds saw the provider refresh them within four
  minutes on a felt-temperature drift. Versioned on every decimal, the same weather was
  interpreted again at every run, and a run whose re-read came after a refresh stopped
  on `evidence_changed` with its context call already paid — three runs of eight that
  evening stopped on it, the weather being the one admitted source measured to move
  within a run's span (the location's version is rounded, an e-mail is re-read by its
  own detail). The version now rounds what decides a change (whole degrees,
  humidity by five points, a precipitation chance by tenths), as the location's rounds
  its coordinates; the excerpt the model reads keeps every value
  ([weather](../../apps/api/src/domains/connectors/discussion_weather.py)). Every such
  stop now logs the source that moved (`discussion_evidence_changed`), never its
  content.

## Amendment 2026-10-22 — a truncated page says nothing of what it did not reach

A cold review found amendment c's rule (only a page that was read and no longer holds
the fact at its version is `evidence_changed`) broken by its own re-read: facts share one page
of their source, read newest first up to `DISCUSSION_REREAD_ITEM_LIMIT` items, and a
fact found by a search or deep in a history window — a person's message two months
back, among hundreds since — lay beyond that page, so a fact nobody changed stopped
the run as `evidence_changed`, after its context calls were paid, at every run that
selected it. A fact a truncated page stopped before is now read again around its own
instant, the way a stored proof is
([revalidation](../../apps/api/src/infrastructure/proactive/discussion_revalidation.py));
still unreached, the run stops as `evidence_page_missing`, never as a change.
