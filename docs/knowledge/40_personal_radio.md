# Personal radio

## What is my radio?
An on-demand spoken programme about your day and the world. The station uses
your account language, the parts of your day you allow it to consult, public
outlets curated by the instance and the sites you add. A fixed grid chooses
what comes next; models write the words and voices speak them. Each news item
shows its source, so you can distinguish a report from the station's commentary.

## How do I start and stop it?
Use the player in the header, the dashboard card or **Radio** page. You start a
session yourself; nothing is produced on a schedule. The player starts after a
short preparation, shows spending as it goes, and stops when you stop it or its
timer expires. It also stops after prolonged absence or pause. The programme
is not stored for replay. If the instance has disabled radio or has no usable
voice, the interface tells you.

## What can I choose?
Choose the parts of your day the radio may read, news subjects and sources, the
sites you add, voices and personality, and a verification level: off, news, or
all. The model verification is off unless you choose it, because each pass
costs extra and can delay the listening; the settings describe it before you
choose. You can pause a site without deleting it.

You choose the voices of LIA, the anchor and each of the three commentators of
debates and discussions — automatic by default. A commentator never shares a
voice with LIA, the anchor or another commentator: the settings grey those
voices out. Your choices are kept when the administrator changes the model of
the same provider, and on the engines that voice several speakers a debate is
played in one take.
Each voice you choose plays a short sample; a radio that is playing pauses so
you can hear it.

When a session starts and you have your own Brave or Perplexity key, up to
five of your strongest interests are searched with it, and every story found
can go on air. When you listen in company, nothing about your interests is
read or broadcast.

## How are sources, privacy and cost handled?
The newsroom collects public pages once for the instance. Your personal
antenna combines only material allowed by your settings; it never shares your
programme with another account. Untrusted news text is treated as data, and
scripts without usable source references are refused. Writing, checking,
translation and speech are charged to your account under the published radio
budget. The player shows the sources and costs rather than presenting the
station's words as an uncited fact.

## Why might the radio stop or skip an item?
The timer, a spending limit, a missing voice, an unavailable source or repeated
production failures may end or limit a session. The player reports the reason.
A short public report can still use its title or summary when the source has no
readable full article; the station does not pretend it read text it could not
obtain.

## Does Radio include commercial content or the Simli avatar?

Start Radio from the header, dashboard or Radio page if your instance offers it. Choose topics, sources, voices and a verification level. It speaks about your day and cited news only while you listen, shows sources and spending, and ends at your timer; there is no replay or scheduled edition.

Advertising, sponsorships, deals and purchase pitches are filtered. Genuine invoices, reservations and editorial, scientific or economic reporting remain eligible. A speaking Simli avatar does not accompany Radio.

## How does the first programme become available?

The first programme is prioritised so listening can start before the remainder is ready. Source availability, synthesis latency and playback still depend on configuration and providers; this is not a fixed latency promise. Native companions and the experimental Simli video connector remain separate.
