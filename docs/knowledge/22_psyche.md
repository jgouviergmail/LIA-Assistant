# Psyche Engine — Emotional Intelligence

## What is LIA's psyche?

An inner state LIA carries from one message to the next — a personality, a mood, passing emotions, a relationship with you — that colours **how** it writes: word choice, rhythm, warmth, how much it suggests. It is a simulation that shapes a style, not human feeling.

**🎭 Felt, never announced:**
• LIA never says "I feel happy": the mood shows in the wording, not in a statement
• it never projects its state onto you — no "you seem tense today" drawn from its own mood
• the facts never change: on a factual, urgent or technical question the colouring recedes, and it never guilt-trips or lectures you

**💬 Just ask:** if you ask how it is, it answers in plain words, without numbers.
• *How are you feeling today?*

The psyche is on by default; switch it off in **Settings › Features › LIA's psyche**. Off, its mood stops evolving and no longer shapes its answers — nothing already observed is erased.

## What are LIA's personality traits, mood, emotions, relationship and drives?

Five layers, each moving at its own pace:

• **Personality traits** (permanent) — the five-trait profile of the personality you chose: how strongly LIA reacts, how much it mirrors your emotions, how quickly it calms down
• **Mood** (hours) — a point on three axes, pleasure, energy and sense of control, read as one of fourteen moods such as serene, curious, playful or melancholic; without stimulus it drifts back to its resting point, with a light time-of-day rhythm
• **Emotions** (minutes) — a few at a time among twenty-two, from joy and curiosity to concern or relief; each one fades and nudges the mood
• **Relationship** (weeks, months) — four stages, Orientation, Exploratory, Affective and Stable, with depth, warmth and trust; the stage only moves forward, and a short message in the chat celebrates each new one
• **Drives** — curiosity and engagement, which make LIA explore other angles or go into more depth

The twenty-two emotions are: joy, gratitude, curiosity, serenity, pride, frustration, concern, melancholy, surprise, amusement, empathy, enthusiasm, confusion, disappointment, tenderness, determination, playfulness, protectiveness, relief, nervousness, wonder and resolve. How many can be active at once is set by your administrator. Every one of the fourteen moods is reachable, at rest as in conversation.

The **Understanding the Psyche** panel explains each mechanism in detail.

## How does the psyche evolve from one answer to the next?

Before each answer, LIA loads its state, lets it drift back toward its personality's resting point for the time that passed, takes the time of day into account, and turns the result into a few directions on tone for that answer.

After each answer, LIA assesses the exchange in a short note you never see — which emotion it triggered, how the exchange went — and that assessment feeds the next turn. It shapes LIA's mood, emotions and relationship; what LIA is good at is measured separately, from evidence (see **Self-efficacy**).

## Where do I see LIA's mood?

**💬 In the chat:** on a wide enough screen, LIA's avatar beside each answer — the companion you chose in **Avatar style** — wears the expression of its mood inside a ring coloured by that mood. The latest answer is alive; older ones keep the mood LIA had when it wrote them and wake up when you hover them. On a computer, hovering also shows the relationship stage, the mood and the active emotions. A change of mood makes the ring pulse once, and a rare, strong emotion releases a few particles — once.

**👀 On the companion:** its resting life — breathing, blinking, small gestures — follows the mood, while the face it makes after an answer follows that answer's tone.

**🔊 Beyond the chat:** spoken comments, Live sessions and proactive notifications carry the same state; with some voice engines, the delivery itself follows the mood, within limits.

Your system's reduced-motion preference keeps all of this still.

## How does an animated companion differ from a personality?

The companion is the visual style you choose: BIP and the other native designs are original. Expressions follow activity and the answer's tone. Personality controls the way LIA converses; it is not an avatar. Native companions move from prepared drawings, so their hidden sides are approximations. Animation needs no image-generation call, and reduced or static display remains available.

## Which psyche settings can I change?

In **Settings › Features › LIA's psyche**:

• the **switch next to the title** turns the whole psyche on or off
• **Psyche settings**: **Show emotional avatar** (the mood on the avatar beside LIA's answers), **Expressiveness** — how strongly emotions colour the answers, from stoic to highly expressive — and **Mood Stability** — how quickly the mood returns to its resting point, from volatile to very stable
• **Resets**: **Refresh mood** or **Reset everything**
• **Refresh** re-reads the current state and rewrites LIA's perspective

**💬 From the chat:** the avatar, expressiveness and stability change directly; switching the psyche on or off, or resetting it, asks for your confirmation first.
• *Lower your expressiveness to 30%*
• *Show me your psyche settings*

## What is the difference between "Refresh mood" and "Reset everything"?

Both sit in the **Resets** panel and ask you to confirm.

**🔄 Refresh mood** brings the mood back to its personality's resting point and clears the active emotions and the drives. The relationship, the personality traits and the experience shown in **Self-efficacy** stay.

**🧹 Reset everything** also resets the relationship — stage, depth, warmth, trust — and the Self-efficacy experience: like a first meeting. It cannot be undone.

**✅ Never touched by either:** the personality traits, your memories and the journals. The evolution charts keep their history and mark each reset.

## What does the "LIA's psyche" page show?

When the psyche is on, below the settings:

• **LIA's perspective** — a few sentences in which LIA describes its current state, written by an AI model when you open the page or press Refresh
• **Mood and emotions** — the active emotions as rings and their intensity, and the mood as a point on its three axes
• the five **personality traits** inherited from your chosen personality
• **Your relationship** — stage, depth, warmth, trust and the number of interactions — and **What drives her**: curiosity and engagement, LIA's tendencies, not a measure of you
• **Self-efficacy** — what LIA has learned from its own work
• **LIA's evolution** — four charts over 24 hours, 7, 30 or 90 days: mood, emotions, relationship and drives. Resets are marked, and a missing value is never drawn as zero. Your administrator decides how long this history is kept.

## What does the "Self-efficacy" panel of LIA's psyche show?

In **Settings › LIA's psyche**, this panel shows what LIA has learned from its own work, domain by domain — planning, information, emotional support, creativity, technical, social, organization.

**📏 Two measures, never mixed:**
• **Results**: what an answer truly established against what you asked — never the mere fact that a tool replied
• **Usefulness**: what you expressed with 👍/👎 on the final answer

A pending confirmation, a quota or a service that is not connected never counts as a failure.

**🌱 No index yet, learning, observed:**
An index appears only after several recent observations: a single exchange makes neither a strength nor a weakness. Each index says what it rests on; it is not a probability of being right.

**🧭 What it changes:**
Where evidence is thin, LIA may check a point the answer left open, ask a single question or name what it could not verify. It changes no model, no tool and no budget, and asks you no extra confirmation. LIA's own self-assessment keeps feeding its mood, its emotions and the relationship — never a competence.

**🔒 Private:**
This evidence stays in your account, goes with your export and is erased by a full psyche reset.

## What does LIA's psyche cost?

Very little:

• **Per message**: a short description of LIA's state is added to the request, and LIA ends its answer with a brief self-assessment you never see. Both ride on the answer's own model call — no extra call — and count in your usual consumption
• **LIA's perspective** on the settings page is one small model call each time it is written (opening the page, Refresh), counted on your account like any other
• **Self-efficacy**: where evidence is thin, LIA may make one extra authorised check, within the turn's usual limits

Switched off, the psyche adds nothing to your messages.

## Does my personality choice change LIA's psyche?

Yes. Each personality in **LIA Style** carries its own five-trait profile, which sets LIA's resting mood, how strongly it reacts, how much it mirrors your emotions and how quickly it recovers. An account that never chose a personality follows the instance's default one.

When you switch personality, the traits are updated and the mood moves at once to the new personality's resting point. The relationship you have built and the experience LIA gathered stay as they are.

The personality decides how LIA talks; the psyche decides how that voice is coloured at a given moment.
