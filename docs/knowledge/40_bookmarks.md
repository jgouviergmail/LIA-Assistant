# Bookmarks — the answers you keep

## How do I keep an answer so it outlives the conversation?
Every assistant bubble carries a **bookmark** next to « copy ». One click keeps
the answer — a copy taken at that instant, with the request that produced it
and the date — so it survives a conversation reset. The icon fills when the
answer is kept; a second click lets it go.

## Where do kept answers live?
In **Settings › My generated files › Bookmarks**, newest answer first, with a
search over the answers and the requests. Each card renders the answer with its
formatting — markdown and rich documents alike — and offers three actions:
share (where your device has a share sheet), download as Markdown (your request
quoted above the answer), delete after a confirmation.

## What exactly is kept?
A **copy**, never a pointer. The answer as it was written, the last request of
yours that came before it, and the date the answer was written. A message LIA
sent on its own initiative — a proactive notification — keeps no request,
because your previous words did not produce it. While the conversation lives
the bubble still knows the answer is kept; once the conversation is reset, the
bookmark stays whole.

## Is there a limit?
Yes: an account keeps a bounded number of bookmarks, and the tab shows that
limit next to the exact count. Keeping the same answer twice never counts twice.

## Do bookmarks leave with my account?
They travel with your account export and are deleted with your account — never
by a conversation reset, which removes the messages you exchanged and nothing
you chose to keep.

## Can an administrator turn this off?
A **Bookmarks** switch exists in the capability panel. Off, the bookmark action
disappears from the bubbles; what was already kept stays readable, exportable
and deletable — a switch removes the ability, never the record.
