# Bookmarks — the answers you keep

## How do I keep an answer so it outlives the conversation?

Every assistant bubble carries a **bookmark** next to the copy button (**Keep this answer**). One click keeps the answer — a copy taken at that instant, with the request that produced it and the date — so it survives a conversation reset. The icon fills when the answer is kept; a second click lets it go.

Kept answers live in **Settings › Preferences › My generated files › Bookmarks**, newest first, with a search over answers and requests. Each card renders the answer with its formatting and offers four actions: share (where your device has a share sheet), download as Markdown (your request quoted above the answer), send by e-mail (where your instance offers it), and delete after a confirmation — which leaves the conversation untouched.

An account keeps a bounded number of bookmarks; the tab shows the count against the limit. Bookmarks travel with your account export and are deleted with your account — never by a conversation reset.

**💶 What it cost:** a card shows up to two named costs — what the answer cost to produce (the same figure as under the chat bubble) and what indexing it in your knowledge space cost, as actually billed.

## What exactly does a bookmark keep?

A **copy**, never a link to the message:
• the answer exactly as LIA wrote it, formatting included — a rich HTML answer stays one
• your request: the last message you wrote before that answer, quoted above it
• the date the answer was written (**Answered on …**)

A message LIA sent on its own initiative — a notification — keeps no request, and the card says so rather than borrowing your previous words.

Because it is a copy, resetting the conversation changes nothing to the bookmark, and deleting the bookmark changes nothing in the conversation. Keeping the same answer twice never makes two bookmarks, nor counts twice against your limit.

## Can I send a kept answer by e-mail?

Yes, with **Send by e-mail** on its card, where your instance offers e-mail sharing: the answer goes as the same Markdown file the download gives you, with the subject and the words you choose. It leaves from your connected mailbox, or, without one, goes to your own verified address.

## Can LIA use my kept answers later?

Yes. Where knowledge spaces are available, each kept answer is also indexed in a space of your own, **Kept answers**, created the first time you keep one. When a later question concerns it, LIA can find it and draw on it, in both modes — weeks later included. Your personal radio and the portrait LIA writes of you can read your kept answers too.

**🏷️ The card says where indexing stands:**
• **Indexing…**, then **Indexed in your knowledge space**
• **Indexing deferred (usage limit)** or **Indexing off** — it resumes on its own once allowed again
• **Indexing failed** — remove the bookmark and keep the answer again to retry

The space does not count against your space and document limits. You can rename it or switch it off like any space, but not delete it by hand, and its documents cannot be moved or deleted from it: deleting a bookmark removes its document.

## Can I ask LIA to manage my kept answers?

Yes, where your administrator allows LIA to manage the application (**LIA settings and management**): LIA can list your kept answers, keep an answer, delete one, prepare its download or send it by e-mail — anything that changes something only after you confirm exactly what it shows you.

## Can an administrator turn bookmarks off?

Partly, and a switch never deletes what you kept. With the **Bookmarks** switch off in **Settings › Administration › Platform capabilities**, the bookmark leaves the answers, so a new one can no longer be kept; what you kept stays readable, downloadable and deletable in **My generated files › Bookmarks**.

If the server's configuration leaves bookmarks out altogether, neither the bookmark nor its tab appears; nothing already kept is deleted.
