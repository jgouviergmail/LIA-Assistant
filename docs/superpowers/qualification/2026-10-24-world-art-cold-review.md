# World artistic completion: cold review

## Boundary

The art passes preserve native world geometry, scene identity, collision and
admission, private account portals, direct text and native billing. All artwork
is original procedural Canvas art; no reference-project source or assets were
copied. No renderer/dependency migration or model prompt was added.

## Adversarial findings and corrections

- Flat repeated faces and diagonal ground repetition: shared sculpted lighting,
  restrained material detail and small deterministic style families replace
  them. Detail is baked once, never regenerated per animation frame.
- A still-generic studio: original landscape, abstract and night compositions
  make the creative objects distinct. The brass optics have splayed tripod legs.
- Rotating faces and clothing: human detail rotates with the body; eight walk
  phases and all public tints have real-browser silhouette/signature checks.
- A hair-cap layering artefact was visible in the contact sheet: the lower hair
  pieces now precede the cap. This was caught by visual inspection beyond tests.
- New detail could break actor occlusion: colour and foreground sprites share
  the same bounded style key. Real pixel checks cover the full authored private
  motif/palette catalogue, including solid-alpha agreement and clipping.
- Private appearance leakage through caches: private and neutral families keep
  different prefixes, motifs/palettes are part of identity, and the existing
  keyed owner lifecycle and disposal remain authoritative. The pure family
  contracts have a strict ratchet; no alias or profile content enters art keys.
- Halo cost and edge popping: one cached halo is reused through a typed light
  table; visibility uses actual zoomed bounds. Windows are decorative light,
  not invented application-wide presence evidence.
- Development publication must hydrate, not only return HTTP 200: its explicit
  loopback dev-origin setting is retained and the published URL is exercised
  with authenticated entry, scene, touch viewport and zoom. Old outputs remain
  separate from inactive candidate builds.
- A gallery test had a misencoded accented label: the test was repaired without
  changing application text or weakening its assertion. Text edits use UTF-8.

A native Firefox run also exposed a recovery race: after a foreign owner was
confirmed, focus/visibility still launched identity reads and could remove the
focused reload action before its click. A new unit regression failed on three
reads instead of one. Verification now stops once that foreign identity is
confirmed, retaining the fail-closed screen until explicit document reload.
The other initial Firefox failure occurred before send, while choosing a peer;
six isolated bilingual repetitions (including real identity reads delayed by
250 ms) passed. This initial failure remains recorded rather than counted green;
the unchanged native journeys are replayed after the recovery correction.

A heavily concurrent follow-up reported a WebKit fetch error during the climate
loop's successive hard navigations. Weather conditions now have independent
fixture contexts, retaining the same page-error and rendering assertions. This
avoids carrying pending previous-document routes between climate cases. No
browser error is ignored to obtain a pass; the original failure log is retained.

## Verification strategy

The hermetic art oracle compiles trusted local modules into literal factories
under its own restrictive CSP. It permits only relative imports inside the world
source tree, has no production route/global and never calls an application API.
Application galleries exercise the real renderer, minimap, input, native private
clock/weather adapter and decoded texture accounting. Contact sheets require
visual inspection. The small art-family test suite covers decisions rather than
asserting arbitrary colour constants.

The final frontend suite and shrink-only gates supplement the affected browser
journeys. Native proof journeys use disposable SQL/Redis and inert accounts;
they remain separate from the API-blocking hermetic harness. Performance samples
are taken on optimized output without compilation or full suites running.
Source digests distinguish host, candidate and the published preview.

## Acceptance limits

The decoded RGBA estimate is not browser RSS or complete GPU allocation. FPS and
JavaScript draw p95 are different measurements. Emulated viewport/CPU results do
not certify physical phones, Raspberry Pi thermals or battery use. Actual provider
translation fidelity is unchanged by the art pass and remains separately
qualified. Owner approval of the final aesthetic remains subjective.

## Final evidence

[The final evidence](assets/world-art-f12/evidence.json) records source equality,
coverage, native exact-case replay, per-engine captures and optimized samples.
The final native matrix has one late capacity timeout retained in its primary
report; that exact Firefox case passed when run alone, with no changed application
budgets. The earlier failed run had reached its empty accessibility report.
Weather cases retain independent fixtures and strict error assertions.
WebKit was also compared to the preserved previous build: its lower, variable
frame delivery occurs on this bench with the older renderer as well. These
results do not establish a regression or certify physical Safari performance.
