# Avatar finishing direction

## Approved scope

The owner approved a visible finishing pass on every retained avatar and both
variants, without a heavy redesign. The owner additionally requests removal of
basic rotations, pointless rocking and other amateur-looking decorative motion,
while preserving breathing and relevant expressions. Video review and tests are
required; sampled poses alone do not establish perceived fluidity.

## Direction

- Preserve the current source artwork, geometry registrations, scarf fittings,
  native rig, lifecycle and single clock. Add no dependency or public route.
- Remove autonomous whole-body rolls, somersaults, repetitive jigging and generic
  idle tilt/bounce flourishes. Keep purposeful gaze, acknowledgment, listening,
  thought, speech, breath, surprise, bow, sneeze and sleep where already allowed.
- Give each character a coherent movement vocabulary: deliberate Oscar,
  precise and alert robots, soft cloud/organic movement, quiet screen characters.
  Refine existing anticipation, held poses and recoveries rather than adding
  independent random motion or increasing the overall event rate.
- Preserve facial emotion and speech ownership. Idle scenes yield immediately
  to work, speech and interaction without a position or velocity discontinuity.
- Refine material response by existing character/material family, preserving
  source colours and alpha. A scarf's attached band must stay fitted; only its
  free end may respond with bounded secondary motion on the native clock.
- Keep all variants, static portraits, reduced motion, hidden-tab suspension,
  small viewports and graphics-failure recovery coherent.

## Acceptance

Capture immutable before evidence and comparable after videos at native speed
for every retained family and variant. Cover quiet presence, conversation states,
purposeful scenes and interruption/recovery. Inspect at actual display size and
preserve recording/frame diagnostics; report the tested environment honestly.
Retain only changes with a visible benefit in those comparisons.

Behavioral tests must prove retired motion cannot be selected or explicitly
played, breathing remains nonzero, purposeful motion remains reachable, speech
and scene exits settle continuously, and material/cloth contracts still hold.
Run fresh frontend static and full behavioral/coverage gates once source is
stable, with source fingerprints and concurrent-work limits. No weakened test
thresholds, no Git operation, no deployment, and no unrelated edit.
