intermediate approval. Existing world geometry, navigation, server authority,

## Direction

A warm garden campus, with limestone, glazed teal, oak, deep foliage and copper.
Lighting comes consistently from the upper left. Texture describes materials
rather than adding uniform noise; silhouettes stay readable at play zoom.
Use richer cached procedural art, not a new renderer or per-frame decoration.
Keep the original LIA atlas and its native eyes/mouth rig.
