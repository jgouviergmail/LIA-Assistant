# and niquests falls back to urllib3_future automatically. Every installer of
# the lockfiles must therefore export URLLIB3_NO_OVERRIDE=1 (Taskfile setup,
# Dockerfiles, CI). Guard: tests/unit/test_urllib3_namespace_guard.py.
