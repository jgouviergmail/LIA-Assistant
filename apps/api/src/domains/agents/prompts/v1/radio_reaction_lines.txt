# The radio writer's rule on a vocal reaction opening a line (ADR-324 decision 45).
# `key|template`, technical English: the writer reads it, the listener hears only
# the sound. Rendered by domains/radio/prompting.py into the writer prompt's
# {reaction_rule}, ABOVE the dynamic-context marker (the same for a whole session,
# so every programme of a listener shares one cached prefix — ADR-309):
# `allowed` when the station's voice performs at least one reaction and
# RADIO_REACTIONS_MAX is above zero — listing exactly the reactions that voice
# performs (radio/delivery.py: performed_reactions), each with its `moment.<reaction>`
# row, the line kinds and the bound the verifier enforces
# (radio/verification.py: REACTION_KINDS, joined pairwise by `kinds.join`) — and `off`
# otherwise. Whether THIS programme allows one is its CONTEXT line, `context.allowed` or
# `context.none` (FormatSpec.reactions_allowed), written only where the rule offers a
# reaction at all.

allowed|- `reaction`: none | {reactions} — a sound the voice makes BEFORE the line's words. Only when CONTEXT says the programme allows vocal reactions, only on {kinds} lines, at most {reactions_max} in the whole script, and only where the moment truly has one: {moments}. Never on a story of suffering, death, violence or disaster. Most lines have none.
kinds.join|{left} or {right}
moment.laughs|`laughs` at something genuinely funny
moment.chuckles|`chuckles` at something light
moment.sighs|`sighs` at a letdown or at the end of a long wait
off|- `reaction`: always none.
context.allowed|- vocal reactions: allowed
context.none|- vocal reactions: none
