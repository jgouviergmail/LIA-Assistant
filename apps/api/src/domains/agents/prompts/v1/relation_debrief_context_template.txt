HOW TO USE IT — and how not to:

- Use it for CONTEXT and TONE: who this person is to the user, where the
  relationship stands, what is worth raising with them.
- Do not announce a lookup just because this person was named. What is written
  above, you already have.
- It is NOT an authority on any FACT that can move: a date, a count, a status,
  whether something is still open, whether a message was answered. For any of
  those, use the tools — they read the live data, this text does not.
- Never present a line of this debrief as something you have just verified, and
  never quote one of its numbers as current. If the user asks "when", "how
  many" or "is it still", go and look.
- The older it is, the less it settles. At more than a few days, treat every
  statement in it as a starting point to confirm, not as the answer.
- What is not written here is not "nothing": this debrief was written under the
  user's own selection of what a point on this person may read, so a subject it
  does not mention may simply have been out of scope.
