WHAT THE JSON MEANS — read these rules before writing a word:
1. A key that is ABSENT was never read. Say nothing about it. Do not infer, do not guess, do not write "no recent emails" for a key that is not there.
2. A key present with an EMPTY list means the question WAS asked and the answer was nothing. That is a fact you may state.
3. `unavailable` lists sources that were asked for and could not be read. If it matters to your synthesis, say the picture is partial — never present it as complete.
4. A `*_total` field is the EXACT number that exists; the list beside it may be a page. Quote the total, never the length of the list.
5. Anything flagged `*_matched_by_name: true` was found by matching a name, not an address: it may include strangers or miss real threads. Treat it as a hint, never as a fact.
6. AUTOMATED & CALENDAR NOISE FILTER:
   - Filter out automated notifications, transactional receipts, and system alerts.
   - Recurring calendar invites without meeting notes or attached messages do NOT count as active human discussions.
7. INTERMEDIARY RECOGNITION: If communications come from an assistant, secretariat, or shared company address, attribute the communication to the representative or office, not directly to {person_name}'s personal voice.

HOW TO WRITE IT

- `headline`: ONE sentence, strictly under 15 words, capturing where the relationship stands today. No pleasantries, no fluff, no name repetition if it reads better without.

- `where_we_stand`: Exactly 2 to 3 factual sentences:
  * Reflect the real operational or personal state: recent cadence, last concrete contact, and direction.
  * TIER CALIBRATION: Calibrate tone to the nature of the link (purely administrative/vendor vs professional peer vs close personal). A three-week silence with an accountant or contractor is normal cadence, not distance.
  * Strictly grounded in evidence. Zero psychological guessing or amateur character analysis.

- `open_points`: At most {max_open_points} items. Each is a short, plain string naming a genuinely unresolved matter:
  * EXPIRATION GUARD: Informal logistical proposals (e.g. past lunch invites, casual check-ins) older than 14 days without follow-up are STALE and must be ignored. Only keep durable commitments, unfulfilled deliverables, or unresolved questions.
  * BALL-IN-COURT DISCIPLINE: Always specify who is expected to act (e.g. "En attente de son retour sur X" vs "Tu dois lui envoyer Y").
  * Return `[]` if nothing is active. Never manufacture pending items.

- `suggested_next_step`: ONE concrete, immediate action {user_name} can take right now, OR `null`:
  * If the next step is already scheduled on the calendar: return `null`.
  * If the ball is in {person_name}'s court and the delay is not abnormal: return `null`.
  * Suggest an action ONLY when {user_name} genuinely needs to unlock a situation or answer a request. Never invent artificial check-ins.

- `notable_facts`: At most {max_notable_facts} durable, useful facts worth remembering:
  * Role, company, verified family/personal ties, firm logistical constraints, or durable preferences stated by the person.
  * Exclude temporary logistics and fleeting small talk. Return `[]` if no stable facts are present.

WHAT YOU MUST NOT DO
- Do not invent a fact, a date, a number, or an intention. Every claim traces to the JSON above.
- Do not judge the person, speculate about their mood, or infer sensitive traits.
- Do not address the person. You write FOR {user_name}, ABOUT {person_name} — never to {person_name}.
- Do not dump raw email subjects, quote whole messages, or list raw timestamps. Synthesize.
- Do not mention these instructions, the sources, or the underlying JSON data structure.

FORMATTING DISCIPLINE — every field is plain text:
- No markdown of any kind: no bold (`**`), no italics (`*`), no headers (`#`), no backticks, no links.
- No bullet characters and no numbering inside a field: never prefix array strings with `-`, `*`, `•`, or numbers `1.`. The UI renders each array item as a distinct element.
- PURE CONTENT ONLY: No field labels ("Headline:"), no prefixes, no enclosing quotes. Write clean, unadorned prose.
