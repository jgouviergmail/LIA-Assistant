# The pictures a writer may ask for, read in its request's tail: none, or the
# rules its person's preference allows, under the budget normalization enforces.
illustrations.none|ILLUSTRATIONS: none for this document. Leave every `illustration` empty and `image_style` empty.
illustrations.auto.slides|ILLUSTRATIONS: ask for at most {illustrations_max} pictures, only where a picture serves the reader: the cover first, then part openers, a content slide only when an image explains better than words; never for data, a procedure or a dense slide, and none at all for a short or plain deck. For each, `illustration.subject` describes the scene in English (what is shown, never any text in it) and `illustration.alt` describes it in the document's language; give the deck ONE `image_style` in English (medium, palette, mood) that every picture shares.
illustrations.on_request.slides|ILLUSTRATIONS: only if the request asks for images, pictures or visuals; then at most {illustrations_max} pictures, where the request wants them (the cover, part openers, content slides). For each, `illustration.subject` describes the scene in English (what is shown, never any text in it) and `illustration.alt` describes it in the document's language; give the deck ONE `image_style` in English (medium, palette, mood) that every picture shares. Otherwise leave every `illustration` and `image_style` empty.
illustrations.auto.sectioned|ILLUSTRATIONS: ask for at most {illustrations_max} pictures, only where a picture serves the reader: the document's cover `illustration` first, then an `illustration` block opening a part when an image explains better than words; never for data, a procedure, a contract or a note, and none at all for a short or plain document. For each, `illustration.subject` describes the scene in English (what is shown, never any text in it) and `illustration.alt` describes it in the document's language, and an illustration block's `caption` names it; give the document ONE `image_style` in English (medium, palette, mood) that every picture shares.
illustrations.on_request.sectioned|ILLUSTRATIONS: only if the request asks for images, pictures or visuals; then at most {illustrations_max} pictures, where the request wants them (the document's cover `illustration`, `illustration` blocks). For each, `illustration.subject` describes the scene in English (what is shown, never any text in it) and `illustration.alt` describes it in the document's language, and an illustration block's `caption` names it; give the document ONE `image_style` in English (medium, palette, mood) that every picture shares. Otherwise leave every `illustration` and `image_style` empty.
