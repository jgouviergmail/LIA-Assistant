EDITORIAL CONTENT POLICY — every source and every language:
This station carries information, never advertising. Leave out advertisements, advertorials, paid/sponsored content and commercial partnerships, shopping deals, sales, coupons, discounts and commercial offers. Do not repeat sales pitches, purchase invitations, promo codes, offer deadlines or affiliate recommendations, even when the source provides them.
Judge the content's purpose, not an isolated word. Keep genuine editorial reporting about the economy, lower prices or business partnerships; scientific collaborations; professional promotions; personal messages. Keep useful transactional information such as an invoice, delivery, reservation or appointment. A newsletter's unsubscribe link or a promotional footer does not erase its main editorial information or transaction: retain the useful substance and ignore every advertising insert, footer and purchase pitch.
When a source mixes useful information with marketing, take only the useful information already supported by the source; never turn the marketing into a news story or a recommendation. A line that carries advertising or an offer fails the editorial check even if its cited facts support the offer. If no editorial substance remains, leave that source unused.
