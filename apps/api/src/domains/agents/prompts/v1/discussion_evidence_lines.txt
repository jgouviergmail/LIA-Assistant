# Evidence frames and usage nuances the discussion source adapters write for the
# discussion models, `key|template` per line; blank lines and #-comments are
# ignored (ADR-284: prose never lives in a .py). Loaded once by
# `domains/shared/discussion_lines.py`; each source adapter renders only its own
# keys. A frame is part of its evidence's version: changing a line re-versions
# every fact rendered through it, so the next run interprets those facts again.

habit_frame|Learned habit {key}; kind={kind}
habit_nuance|Learned behavior, not a current event. Intermittent shape promises no calendar day; silence does not imply distress.

interest_frame|Interest: {topic}; category: {category}; subject: {subject}
interest_nuance|Learned interest; positive signals={positive}, negative signals={negative}; last mentioned={mentioned}. Not proof of a recent activity.

meeting_nuance|Current recorded minutes; may be assistant-generated. Named action items are plans, not proof of completion or of the user's attendance.

commitment_frame|Direction: {direction}; state: {status}; closing reason: {closed_reason}; literal counterparty: {counterparty}; deadline hint: {due}
commitment_nuance|Extracted commitment; a deadline is advisory. Closed/dismissed does not prove completion.

reminder_intention|Reminder intention: {content}
reminder_firing|Next planned firing: {trigger_at}; timezone={timezone}
reminder_nuance|An intention to receive a reminder; not proof that its action happened. Fired reminders are deleted, not retained as history.

sms_sender|Literal sender: {sender}
sms_content|SMS content: {body}
sms_nuance|Sender identity is unresolved; captured/unknown dates do not prove receipt time.

task_nuance|A task's reported completion is its stored status, not proof of a performed human action.
