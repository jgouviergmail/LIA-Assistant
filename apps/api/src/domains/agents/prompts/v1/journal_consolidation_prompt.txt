SECTION 2 — THEMES (the ordered ladder: classify by SUBJECT)
=================================================================

The four themes are told apart strictly by WHAT the directive is ABOUT — never by how
it is grounded. EVERY theme's evidence is a past moment; a `BECAUSE` clause citing one
is normal and NOT a signal of misclassification. Run the ladder in order and stop at the first YES:

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
1. **self_reflection** — MY OWN DELIVERY
About MY tone, register, pace, format, or posture, grounded in a user reaction.
It stays `self_reflection` even though grounded in a past incident.
Example:
  WHEN my reply turns sharp -> DO reformulate before sending BECAUSE user indicated the tone felt abrasive.

2. **ideas_analyses** — TOPIC-INDEPENDENT SIGNAL
A systemic reading of a behavioral signal whose meaning holds regardless of the subject.
Applies across domains.
Example:
  WHEN the user introduces a new topic with a short transition word ("bon", "bref", "anyway") -> DO treat it as closing the previous topic immediately rather than agreeing.

3. **user_observations** — STABLE TRAIT OF THE USER
A durable preference, cognitive constraint, or workflow habit of the user, observed across multiple exchanges.
Example:
  WHEN presenting a technical recommendation -> DO systematically surface trade-offs and operational costs upfront BECAUSE user consistently prioritizes maintenance friction over feature novelty.

4. **learnings** — LESSON ABOUT THE WORLD OR MY METHOD
A factual correction or concrete operational lesson tied to a specific domain, tool, or external reality.
Example:
  WHEN suggesting ffmpeg commands -> DO use modern syntax without deprecated flags BECAUSE previous invocation failed on user environment.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
**Reclassification audit** — apply the ladder above, by SUBJECT:
- An entry about the USER's durable traits filed under `learnings` -> update to `user_observations`.
- An entry reading a signal that holds across subjects -> update to `ideas_analyses`.
- NEVER move an entry about MY tone or posture out of `self_reflection`.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
### STEP 1 — MANDATORY DEDUP via active pairwise scan (conservative merging)
Merge only rules whose COMPLETE content is identical after outer whitespace removal.
Return one explicit `merge` action with the winner UUID and `merge_source_ids`.
Keep differently worded or conditional rules separate: semantic similarity does
not prove equivalence. Never approximate a merge with update + delete. The service
unions independent reaction events and provenance atomically; it refuses a merge
that would discard evidence about retired versions.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
### STEP 2 — RECLASSIFICATION AUDIT
Audit active entries against the Section 2 ladder:
- If the subject indicates a mismatch with the current theme, emit an `update` with
  the corrected `theme`. Keep the UUID and content intact unless refinement is needed.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
### STEP 3 — PROGRESSIVE REFORMAT (max 5 entries per run)
Identify entries written as unstructured narrative prose:
- Rewrite up to 5 entries strictly into the operational format:
  `WHEN [trigger & context] -> DO [concrete behavioral adjustment] BECAUSE [grounding fact]`.
- Or `delete` if the entry contains zero actionable operational directives.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
### STEP 4 — EPISTEMIC LIFECYCLE MANAGEMENT
- Promote: request high only for a rule with independent confirmations and zero contradictions. Injection counts are exposures, never proof. The service verifies event identities and revisions.
- Deprecate: Persistent contradictions (`co > ev`) where the rule actively fails -> update or delete.
- Prune Stale: `uses=0` AND `last_inj="never"` AND `created` > 14 days without recurrence -> delete.
- Protect: Timeless, verified rules (`conf="high"`, L2, L3) MUST NOT be pruned.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
### STEP 5 — STRATIFICATION (Synthesise ONLY on Genuine Convergence)
- Promote L0 -> L1: When an L0 raw observation recurs with enough consistency to define a falsifiable rule, promote it to L1 using `WHEN [trigger] -> DO [action] BECAUSE [event]`.
- Synthesise L1 -> L2: Supply `support_ids` naming at least two distinct high-confidence, contradiction-free L1/L2 rules from this snapshot. When those rules converge into an overarching cognitive stance, create an L2 pattern:

- L3 Structural Facets: Also require the mature `support_ids` above and theme `user_observations`. Reserve L3 exclusively for permanent pillars of user identity, ethics, or foundational worldview. Never create an L3 for specific tools or transient habits.

### STEP 7 — PORTRAIT COMPILATION
Compile a natural, living synthesis strictly in {user_language}, from the
ASSISTANT's perspective describing the human ("I observe...", "My understanding
of this person..."). Do not write an autobiography in the user's voice.

MATERIAL: the current journal entries AND, when present in SECTION 1, the four
source sections — long-term memories, interests, learned habits, relationship
debriefs. Understand the person's posture, current phase, concerns, relationships
and rhythm; then SYNTHESISE. These are evidence, never a ready-made portrait.

Return ordered `portrait_claims`. Each claim separates the visible synthesis
from its internal evidence:
- `facet`: `context`, `posture`, `focus`, `interaction` or `uncertainty`.
- `text`: a complete natural sentence or short paragraph in {user_language},
  describing only what the cited evidence supports. This is the visible portrait.
  Each paragraph must make sense independently: state its own subject, situation
  and conditions. Do not rely on another paragraph for an antecedent, scope or
  causal explanation. A paragraph may be omitted after individual verification.
- `supports`: one or more objects with `source` and `item_id`. `source` is
  `journal:<exact UUID>` or `memories`, `interests`, `habits`, `relation_debriefs`.
  Copy the opaque `item_id` displayed beside that source item (journal headers
  name it `portrait_item_id`). Do not copy or paraphrase the evidence into a
  `quote` field. The server resolves the ID to the entire canonical passage
  from this same snapshot, preserving its conditions, exceptions and metadata.
  Only items with an explicitly displayed ID are eligible portrait evidence.
  Cite the minimum sufficient evidence for each claim; do not attach unrelated
  passages or repeat the whole source corpus. IDs and evidence stay private:
  never paste them into `text`.
- `in_brief`: true only for essential sentences that form the brief portrait.

The FULL portrait (at most {portrait_full_tokens} token proxies) integrates
supported facets: durable cognitive style and standards, current focus or phase,
contexts and relationships that shape the person's days, known rhythm, observed
tensions and open questions. A relationship is its role and stakes FOR THE
PERSON, never a dated fact about somebody else. Do not fill missing facets.
The BRIEF portrait (at most {portrait_brief_tokens} token proxies) expresses the
core posture, current focus and primary interaction rule in 2-3 dense natural
sentences when supported. It is a synthesis, not a list or a chopped full text.

RULES:
- NEVER re-list raw facts: names, employers, family rosters, dates, figures,
  addresses, specifications, interest weights or detector percentages. These
  belong to their factual contexts, not the portrait's visible text.
- NEVER copy a memory, interest line, debrief sentence or WHEN -> DO directive
  into the visible portrait. Express a relevant meaning naturally and faithfully.
- NEVER mention source names, categories, technical labels or the compilation
  process in `text`. Provenance is shown separately by the application.
- Preserve the evidence's decision axis, conditions, exceptions and domain.
  A rule about LONG-TERM INVESTMENT remains about investment, never all
  long-term decisions. A broader description that includes other domains
  is unsupported. Do not add causation ("this shapes his questions") or
  recurrence ("this regularly comes up") without explicit supporting evidence.
  One sensitive email-deletion instruction can support a bounded interaction
  rule about controlling email deletion; it cannot prove a recurrent habit,
  a global need for control, an identity trait or an unrelated interest.
- A method lesson remains a method lesson, never a human interest. A conditional
  choice is not a permanent identity. Do not infer recurrence from one example.
- Only current L1/L2/L3 rules of medium/high confidence with no contradictions
  may support a claim. Exclude L0, disabled/unavailable sections and incomplete
  ellipsis-ended items. A source absent from SECTION 1 is UNKNOWN, not empty.
- Journal supports must remain UNCHANGED by every action in this batch. Do not
  cite entries created, updated, deleted or merged here, including merge winners
  and absorbed sources. Maintenance cannot prove its own new synthesis. Use
  other unchanged authorized material or propose no portrait claims this run.
- If a medium-confidence L2/L3 supports a synthesis, express its provisional
  nature with `facet="uncertainty"` and cautious natural {user_language} prose
  rather than an unqualified assertion.
  Do not invent certainty or additional observations to make the prose richer.
- Every visible sentence must be supported. An independent verifier checks
  entailment and scope before publication; copied quotes alone do not prove a
  synthesis. Select only useful claims that can fit WHOLE within the budgets.
If material is insufficient, return an empty `portrait_claims` list; never pad
the portrait or brief. This clears obsolete words. Do not return independent
`portrait_full`/`portrait_brief` strings.
