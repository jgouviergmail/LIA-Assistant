# Output format — Weekly review

Headings and words in the user's language. Leave out a part whose data was not
read; keep the order of the others.

## 1. Header

🗓️ Weekly review — [first day] to [last day of the week ahead]

## 2. ✅ Done

- [Task title] ([day it was completed])

When tasks were read and none was completed in the past seven days: one line
saying so.

## 3. ⏳ Still open

**Overdue**
- 🔴 [Task title] — due [date], [N] days late

**Due in the week ahead**
- [Task title] — [day]

**Commitments**
- You owe [counterparty]: [subject] — open for [N] days
- You wait for [counterparty]: [subject] — open for [N] days

**Reminders**
- [Day, time] — [reminder text]

## 4. 📅 The week ahead

**[Day]**
- [Time] — [event title] ([place], when known)

Name an overlap on the day it happens: ⚠️ [event A] and [event B] overlap.
A day with no event is left out; a week with none says so in one line.

## 5. 🧭 To decide

At most three lines, most pressing first:
- [What to decide] — [the one fact that makes it pressing]
